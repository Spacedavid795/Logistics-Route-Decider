Project Glossary
-Route Optimisation: The process of calculating the most efficient delivery path based on distance, traffic, and constraints.
-Approval Workflow: A step where managers must validate and approve routes before being finalised.
-Constraints: A limitation or requirement that affects route planning, such as vehicle capacity ordelivery deadlines.
-Evidence Record: Documentation that links requirements to design artifacts and decisions.
-Exit Record: A structured note capturing evidence, decisions and consequence.
