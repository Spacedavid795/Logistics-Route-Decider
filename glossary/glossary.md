Project Glossary 
This glossary defines key terms used in the Logistics Route Decider project.
-Route Optimisation: The process of calculating the most efficient delivery path based on distance, traffic and constraints.
-Approval Workflow:  A step where managers must validate or approve a suggested route before it is finalised.
-Constraints: A limitation or requirement that affects route planning, such as vehicle capacity or delivery deadlines.
Evidence Record: Documentation that links requirements to design artefacts and decisions.
Exit Record: A structured note capturing evidence, decision, consequence, and next uncertainty at the end of a lab session .
